# 3D Printing Enclosures & Stands (Bambu Lab Ready)

Both projects are designed to pair with 3D printed enclosures that can be printed on Bambu Lab printers (A1 Mini, A1, P1P, P1S, or X1-Carbon).

---

## 1. Unit 1: Desk Dashboard Enclosures

### For "CYD" (Cheap Yellow Display - ESP32-2432S028R)
Search these on **[Bambu MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com)** or **[Printables](https://www.printables.com)**:
- **"CYD Desktop Stand / Case"**: Minimalist angled desk dock with cable channel for rear/bottom USB-C.
- **"CYD Retro Television Enclosure"**: Cute retro 80s monitor with fake knobs and speaker grill (perfect for the built-in speaker).
- **"Gridfinity CYD Mount"**: If you use Gridfinity on your desk or workshop bench, snap it right into your grid.

**Recommended Print Settings:**
- **Material**: PLA Basic or Matte (e.g. Charcoal or White)
- **Layer Height**: 0.16mm - 0.20mm
- **Infill**: 15% Gyroid
- **Supports**: Usually not required if printed face-down.

### For LilyGO T-Display-S3
- **"T-Display-S3 Mini Monitor Case"**: Tiny retro CRT computer monitor housing.
- **"T-Display Snap Case"**: Slim pocket/desk clamshell.

---

## 2. Unit 2: DeskBuddy Cyber-Pet Enclosures

For the cyber-pet, character enclosures bring the personality to life for kids and desk display:

- **"Desktop Mini Robot Head"**: Fits a 0.96" or 1.3" OLED in the face plate with articulated ears or antenna.
- **"BMO Companion Case"**: The classic Game Boy / robot body with arms and legs.
- **"Round Eye Orb"**: If using the GC9A01 1.28" circular display, print a sphere eyeball casing (similar to Portal personality cores or Wheatley).
- **"Desk Pet with Touch Horn / Antenna"**: Run a small wire from ESP32 GPIO 4 (Capacitive Touch) to an exposed metal screw, foil, or brass insert on the head. When touched or petted, the pet reacts and giggles!
